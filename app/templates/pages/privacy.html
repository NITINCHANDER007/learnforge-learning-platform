Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register on DataDrix, we collect your name, email address, and encrypted password. As you use the platform, we store your learning progress (completed lessons), bookmarks, and personal notes.

2. Note Privacy and File Attachments

Personal notes and files uploaded to your student account are private. Only you and authenticated platform administrators can access your private study notes and note attachments.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To display your personal learning dashboard and progress.
  • To remember your theme preferences (stored locally on your device).

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third parties or advertisers.
