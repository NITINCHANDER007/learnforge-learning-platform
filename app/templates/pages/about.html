About LearnForge

Learn. Build. Master.

LearnForge is an LMS-style learning platform inspired by the clarity of W3Schools, the depth of GitBook, and the structured roadmaps of Roadmap.sh. Our mission is simple: provide clean, distraction-free, self-paced programming and data science education.

What Makes LearnForge Different?

  • Markdown-First Philosophy: Lessons are authored as clean Markdown files, ensuring rapid load times and zero advertising bloat.
  • Curated Curriculum: 14 core subjects ranging from Python, SQL, and Web Development to Data Visualization, Machine Learning, and Power BI.
  • Practical Progress Tracking: Keep track of completed lessons, bookmark essential guides, and write personal notes alongside every lesson.
  • Privacy Focused: Your notes and progress are strictly yours. We do not track you across the web.

Technology

LearnForge is built with Flask 3, Jinja2 templates, vanilla CSS with custom design tokens, SQLite/PostgreSQL, and responsive design for every device.
