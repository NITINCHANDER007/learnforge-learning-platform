---
title: Types of Machine Learning
---


- **Supervised learning** — you have labeled data (inputs *and* correct outputs), e.g. predicting house prices from past sales
- **Unsupervised learning** — you only have inputs, and the model finds structure on its own, e.g. grouping customers into segments
- **Reinforcement learning** — an agent learns by trial and error, receiving rewards/penalties, e.g. game-playing AI

Most beginner and business applications start with **supervised learning** — it's the most predictable and easiest to evaluate.
