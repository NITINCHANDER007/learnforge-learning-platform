---
title: Legal Considerations
---


Before scraping any site:

- Check `example.com/robots.txt` — it states which parts of the site scrapers are allowed to access
- Read the site's Terms of Service — many explicitly prohibit scraping
- Never scrape personal or copyrighted data for redistribution without permission
- Add delays between requests — hammering a server with rapid requests can be considered a denial-of-service attack, even unintentionally

> **Note:** Public APIs (when a site offers one) are almost always a better, more stable, and more legally clear-cut choice than scraping HTML directly.
