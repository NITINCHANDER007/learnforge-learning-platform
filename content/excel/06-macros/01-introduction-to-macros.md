---
title: Introduction to Macros
---


A macro records a sequence of actions and lets you replay them with one click — useful for repetitive tasks (formatting a report the same way every week, for example).

**View tab → Macros → Record Macro**, perform your actions, then **Stop Recording**. Run it again anytime via **Macros → Run**.

> **Note:** Macros use VBA (Visual Basic for Applications) under the hood — recording one is the easiest way to start, and you can view/edit the generated code via **Alt+F11** once you're ready to go further than simple recording.
