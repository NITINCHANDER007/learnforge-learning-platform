---
title: Navigating the Interface
---


The **Ribbon** at the top groups commands into tabs (Home, Insert, Formulas, Data...). The **Formula Bar** shows the actual content of the selected cell — useful when a cell displays a calculated result but you need to see the formula behind it.

> **Note:** Clicking a cell shows its *value*; clicking it and looking at the Formula Bar shows its *formula* (if it has one) — these are often different things.
