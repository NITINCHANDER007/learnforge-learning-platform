---
title: Basic Formulas
---


Every formula starts with `=`.

```
=A1+A2        adds two cells
=SUM(A1:A10)  sums a range of cells
=AVERAGE(A1:A10)
=A1*1.1       multiply a cell by a fixed number
```

> **Note:** `A1:A10` is a **range** — everything from A1 through A10 inclusive. Ranges are used in almost every Excel formula that works across multiple cells.
