---
title: Pivot Tables
---


A pivot table summarizes large datasets by dragging fields into **Rows**, **Columns**, and **Values** areas — no formulas required.

**Insert → PivotTable**, then drag e.g. `Department` into Rows and `Salary` into Values (set to Average) to instantly see average salary per department.

> **Note:** Pivot tables don't auto-update when your source data changes — right-click the pivot table and choose **Refresh** after editing the underlying data.
