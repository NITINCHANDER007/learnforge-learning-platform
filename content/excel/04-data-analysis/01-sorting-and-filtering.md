---
title: Sorting and Filtering
---


Select your data range → **Data tab → Sort** (to reorder rows) or **Filter** (to show a dropdown arrow on each column header, letting you show only matching rows).

> **Tip:** Convert your range to a proper **Table** (`Ctrl+T`) first — tables automatically get filter dropdowns, auto-expand as you add rows, and make formulas easier to write and read.
