---
title: Building Dashboards
---


A **report** is a multi-page collection of visuals you design in Power BI Desktop. A **dashboard** (built in the Power BI Service, after publishing) pins key visuals from one or more reports onto a single, high-level summary page.

> **Note:** Dashboards are single-page and read-only for viewers; reports are the fully interactive, multi-page experience underneath them.
