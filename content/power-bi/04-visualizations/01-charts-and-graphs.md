---
title: Charts and Graphs
---


Drag a field onto the canvas, then choose a visual type from the Visualizations pane — bar chart, line chart, pie chart, and dozens more.

> **Tip:** Match the chart type to the question you're answering: trends over time → line chart; comparing categories → bar chart; parts of a whole → pie chart (used sparingly — bar charts are usually clearer for more than 3-4 categories).
