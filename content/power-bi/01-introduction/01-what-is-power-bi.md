---
title: What is Power BI?
---


Power BI is Microsoft's business intelligence tool — it connects to your data sources, lets you model relationships between tables, and builds interactive dashboards without writing code.

The typical workflow: **connect** to data → **model** relationships between tables → **visualize** with charts → **publish** as a shareable report.

> **Note:** Power BI Desktop (for building reports) is free; the Power BI Service (for sharing reports online) has paid tiers for larger teams.
